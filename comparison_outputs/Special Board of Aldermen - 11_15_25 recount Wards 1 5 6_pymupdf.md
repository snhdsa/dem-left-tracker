## DISCUSSION 

- Canvas of Returns on November 4, 2025 Municipal General Election Request for Recount of Votes Cast for Wards 1, 5, and 6 Race 

## President Wilshire 

Mr. Clerk, I’m going to turn it over to you. 

## Dan Healey, City Clerk 

So we’re here today.  We had a recount request for Ward 1, Ward 5, and Ward 6.  We have a meeting scheduled to start at 9 a.m. for those recounts.  I had sent letters to all the candidates and indicated that Ward 5 won’t start until at least 10 a.m. and then Ward 6 won’t start until 11 a.m.   At that time if needed, I’ll go over the recount procedures, again, if more people from those wards show up. 

So what we’re going to be doing here is we have all the boxes from those Wards up here.  These include cast, uncast, absentees.  We’re going to open every box and show what the contents are of those boxes.  If there are fully uncast ballots, we’re going to fan through them.  Each person, each candidate will have the opportunity to have an observer up front to see the fanning through to confirm that they’re uncast.  They’ll go back in the box and be sealed.

The absentee envelopes, we’re fanning through those just to make sure there’s no ballot in there that, you know, shouldn’t be that should be with the cast ballots but the absentee ballots – the opportunity to challenge an actual absentee ballot was on election day.  So there’s no challenges to the envelopes, only to ballots. 

Then the third box, the third set of boxes will be the cast ballots. The procedure that we’re going to use today is that these first two tables will be sorting the ballots based off of what the voter chose.  The first person will read off the name that they believe the ballot to be for, they’ll pass it to the second person that will hold it up for the observers, and after a few seconds, they’ll place it in the box.  One the ballot is placed in that box, once it hits the box, the time for a challenge is over on that ballot.  Periodically, team members, City Clerk staff, and volunteers – Tina Guilford, Derry Town Clerk, and Cassie who works for the City Clerk’s office here in Nashua, and myself will be coming around and swapping out the boxes with the counting table that’s in the back corner of the room.  The two back tables. 

At the counting table, each person will count into stacks of 25 for that candidate or that choice.  The first person will count into 25 and put an elastic around it.  He’ll pass it to the second person who will take the elastic off, count 25, put the elastic back on, and a sticky note indicating that it was counted for a second time.  Once they are finished with that, the ballots will come back to this table.  They’ll be held til the end where myself and staff will go through the piles and tally the results up.  At that time, again, an observer from each candidate can be present. 

If there are any challenges during the process, those will be taken, they’ll be brought up to this table up here, place them in this box here, and they will go to the Aldermen at the end of that Ward, the recount, the Aldermen will rule on the ballots.  We do have one Alderman that will not be able to rule on the Ward 6 ballots so at that time, they’ll recuse themselves from the Ward 6 ballots as they are a candidate but they are able to rule on Ward 1 and Ward 5.  Are there any questions now at this time?
