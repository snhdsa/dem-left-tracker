## CAUCUS OF THE BOARD OF ALDERMEN 

DECEMBER 11, 2025 

City Clerk Dan Healey called the assembly to order at 6:43 p.m. in the aldermanic chamber, and duly noticed in two places, including the City’s website, in accordance with the requirements of RSA 91-A:2 II. 

Prayer was offered by City Clerk Dan Healey; Alderman-at-Large Ben Clemons led in the Pledge to the Flag. 

## City Clerk Healey 

Members of the Board of Aldermen and Aldermen-Elect, we are gathered here this evening to select a President and Vice President of the Board of Aldermen for the next term to begin January 4, 2026.  As in the past, I will conduct the meeting until a President-Elect has been selected at which time the President-Elect will take over conducting the meeting.  The formal election of the President and Vice President of the Board will take place during the Board’s organizational meeting on January 4, 2026 at the Nashua Center for the Arts.
